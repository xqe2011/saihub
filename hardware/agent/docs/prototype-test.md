# Prototype acceptance record — NOT YET EXECUTED

Record board serial/revision, adapter model, cable model/length, firmware commit, ambient temperature and equipment. Attach scope captures and measured values. SAIHub-Mini Rev A is routed and CAD-checked; execute this procedure after fabrication. It uses nominal 1 A current limiting, with a calculated 0.908-1.081 A threshold range. Continuous operation at exactly 1 A is not guaranteed; record each channel’s onset and qualify a continuous load below the lower threshold.

1. **Unpowered inspection.** Verify all fitted parts are on top and the 5020 positive/negative/dummy lands match its drawing. Check D1/D2/D3 polarity, C2/C4 ceramic values and DC-bias characteristics, module orientation, pin-1 header direction, buzzer polarity, USB solder joints and exposed-pad soldering. Verify no VBUS/3.3 V/GND shorts. Fit the external dual-band antenna before RF tests.
2. **First startup.** Use a current-limited laboratory 5 V source first, outputs unloaded. Confirm GPIO8/9 are low from power application through firmware boot; both external rails must remain off under a modest discharge load. Measure always-on 3.3 V at C4 and the module. Then repeat with the actual specified 5 V/3 A adapter and intended cable.
3. **Flashing and recovery.** Connect to a PC with external outputs disabled and no external loads. Verify USB enumeration and flashing in both USB-C orientations. Hold BOOT while applying power, then while pulsing EN/GND reset pads. Verify normal reboot after releasing BOOT. No reset button is fitted.
4. **GPIO mapping.** On J2 pins 5–12, toggle each logical IO0–IO7 and verify only its mapped GPIO responds: 10,1,0,23,4,5,6,24. Exercise input/pull modes and the application's PWM/pulse functions. Use 3.3 V signals only. Check repeated reset does not inadvertently enable external power.
5. **Buzzer.** Use a test firmware PWM on GPIO12 at 4 kHz, 50% duty, for short bursts. Check silence when disabled and during reset; probe the drain to verify flyback clamping. Do not leave the transducer continuously energized with DC. Confirm buzzer operation while both external rails are disabled.
6. **Independent rail switching.** With the specified adapter, exercise OFF/OFF, ON/OFF, OFF/ON, ON/ON. Ramp each electronic load from 0 to 0.85 A independently, then approach 1 A to measure limit onset. Measure rail voltage at J2 and source voltage at USB. Switch each loaded output repeatedly while checking ESP32 uptime and the other output. Measure residual voltage/discharge after turnoff under the intended real load; there is no active discharge circuit.
7. **Current limit and short recovery.** Increase each load gradually and record limiting onset and TP5/TP6 fault assertion. Compare the measured threshold with the calculated 0.908-1.081 A bounds; do not demand 1 A continuous output from a unit whose protection starts below 1 A. With a current-limited source and a suitable shorting fixture, briefly short each output independently. Check current limiting, thermal cycling if applicable, fault release, and recovery after removing the short and toggling enable. Ensure the MCU and other rail recover without damage; record any reset as a failure to investigate. Never short unswitched USB input or always-on 3.3 V.
8. **Simultaneous stress.** Apply 0.85 A to each external rail initially; repeat near 1 A where the measured limit permits, run sustained Wi-Fi traffic on 2.4 and 5 GHz separately, and repeat with buzzer bursts. Test both cold startup into loads and enabling loads after boot. Run at least 30 minutes after temperature approaches equilibrium and repeat at the intended maximum ambient/enclosure condition. Record input current, USB voltage, both output voltages and temperatures of U2, L1, D1, C2/C4 and U4/U5. Input must remain within the 3 A adapter budget without source cycling or brownouts.
9. **SGM6232 acceptance gate.** At no load, each qualified continuous rail load, and simultaneous full loads plus Wi-Fi, capture startup, load-step response, minimum 3.3 V and ripple at C4 and at U3. Use a ground spring and 20 MHz bandwidth limit for ripple; also inspect unrestricted transients. Proposed engineering targets: steady rail 3.135–3.465 V, ripple ≤100 mV peak-to-peak, and no excursion outside the module's published operating supply range after startup. Capture EN timing relative to supply settling. Reject oscillation, failed startup, brownout or excessive temperature; set junction-temperature margins from measured case temperatures and manufacturer thermal data. Do not accept a rail rating merely because it works briefly at room temperature.
10. **Cable and hot-plug margin.** Repeat startup/load tests with the actual intended cable and the worst acceptable cable in the product specification. Capture VBUS and 3.3 V on insertion/removal, including both loads enabled by application software. The input bulk capacitor has no slew limiter; check inrush/source recovery and USB behavior explicitly. Use a data/power fixture with host VBUS isolated if observing USB data during adapter-powered stress; never tie adapter and PC power outputs together.

Record pass/fail for every item and measured limits. If 3.3 V fails under the required load, reduce the declared output current only with an explicit product decision, or revise the regulator/power input design. Verify at least 4.75 V at U2 IN under the qualified load, and separately investigate startup/dropout as input falls toward the 4.5 V specified minimum. CAD validation alone cannot accept the regulator dropout or thermal margin.
